# User Guide

## 1. What NammaTN does

NammaTN helps citizens report civic issues such as water problems, road damage, drainage issues, waste problems, streetlight faults, and flooding. The app understands complaints written in Tamil, Tanglish, or English and helps convert them into a structured complaint.

## 2. How to register

1. Open the application in the browser.
2. Click Register.
3. Enter your full name, email, phone number, and password.
4. Submit the form.

You will then be logged in automatically.

## 3. How to login

1. Click Login.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Press Sign In.

## 4. How to report a civic issue

1. On the Citizen page, describe the problem in plain language.
2. Example messages:
   - "enga street la 3 days ah water varala"
   - "Thiruvaiyaru main road la periya pothole irukku"
   - "Water is not supplied to our area for 3 days"
3. The AI assistant reads the message and suggests the issue type and severity.

## 5. Tamil, Tanglish, and English examples

You can write the complaint in any of these styles:

- Tamil: "enga street la 3 days ah thanni nikkuthu"
- Tanglish: "Thiruvaiyaru main road la pothole irukku"
- English: "Streetlight is not working in our area"

## 6. How the AI understands the complaint

The system reads your complaint and tries to identify:
- the issue category
- how serious the issue is
- whether more location information is needed

It then asks for the district and area so the complaint can be tracked properly.

## 7. How to enter district and area

After the AI summary appears, enter:
- district
- area or local place name
- optional duration such as 3 days

These fields help the authority understand where the issue is happening.

## 8. How to optionally upload a photo

You may attach one proof photo if you have it:
- click Upload file to pick an image
- or click Use camera to capture a photo from the device camera

The image is optional. A complaint can still be submitted without a proof photo.

## 9. How to submit

After you have entered the district and area, click Create Civic Complaint.

The app creates a complaint and returns a complaint ID.

## 10. How to save the complaint ID

Keep the complaint ID safe. This is the number you can use to track your issue.

Example:

TN-WTR-260812-AB12

## 11. How to track a complaint

1. In the complaint tracker section, enter your complaint ID.
2. Click Track.
3. The current status appears on screen.

## 12. Meaning of status values

Submitted
- the complaint has been received and is waiting for review

In Progress
- the authority is working on the issue

Resolved
- the issue has been marked as completed

## 13. What the Admin/Authority does

The authority can:
- sign in to the admin dashboard
- review complaints coming from citizens
- view complaint details and proof photos when uploaded
- change the complaint status as work is done

## 14. Notes

This application is a student-built prototype and is not an official government service.
